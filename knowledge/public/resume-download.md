# How to download my resume from the portfolio agent

If you ask for my resume, CV, or PDF in the chat panel:

1. At the **top of the agent panel**, find **Save on close** (download icon).
2. **Turn the toggle ON** (purple/on state).
3. **Close the chat panel** (X) or **end a voice call** — your browser will download:
   - `Abhishek-Swami-Resume.pdf`
   - `abhishek-chat-YYYY-MM-DD.txt` (this conversation)

Nothing is emailed; files save to your device (laptop or phone downloads folder).

If the toggle is off, closing the panel will not download files.

You can also use the **Resume** link in the site header/social links for a direct PDF.

I am happy to discuss experience, projects, and skills in chat while you grab the file.
