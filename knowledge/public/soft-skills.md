# Soft Skills & Collaboration Style

I'm naturally curious and like learning by building.

When I encounter a new technology, I prefer to understand how it works internally and then build something with it rather than only following tutorials.

I'm comfortable working independently, but I also enjoy collaborating with developers, attending meetups, discussing technical ideas, and learning from other engineers.

## How I work

- I take ownership of the problems I'm working on.
- I learn quickly when working with unfamiliar technologies.
- I prefer practical, hands-on learning.
- I communicate openly when I'm stuck and actively look for solutions.
- I enjoy breaking large problems into smaller, manageable pieces.
- I like experimenting with new ideas and turning them into working prototypes.
- I care about understanding the **why** behind a technical decision, not just implementing it.
