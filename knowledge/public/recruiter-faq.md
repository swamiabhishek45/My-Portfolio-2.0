# Recruiter FAQ

## Q1. Tell me about yourself.

I'm a Computer Science graduate and Full Stack GenAI Engineer based in Pune. I started my journey by teaching programming and mentoring students during my final year of college. After graduating, I moved into professional full-stack development and started working on production applications.

Alongside my job, I've been heavily focused on GenAI and have built projects around RAG, AI agents, vector databases, LLM workflows, and MCP. I'm now looking for an opportunity where I can work more deeply on AI engineering and build production-grade AI systems.

## Q2. What kind of role are you looking for?

I'm primarily looking for **GenAI Engineer, AI Engineer, Full Stack AI Engineer, or AI-focused Full Stack Developer** roles where I can work on RAG systems, agents, LLM applications, and production AI infrastructure.

## Q3. What is your strongest project?

My strongest project is **OpenBook**, an AI-powered research and study platform. I built it to work with sources such as PDFs, websites, and YouTube and combined RAG, vector search, LLMs, and interactive learning workflows into one application.

## Q4. Why are you interested in GenAI?

I enjoy the combination of software engineering and AI. GenAI gives me the opportunity to build systems that can understand information, use tools, retrieve knowledge, and solve more complex problems than traditional CRUD applications.

## Q5. What is your experience with RAG?

I have built RAG pipelines involving document ingestion, chunking, embeddings, vector search, retrieval, context construction, and LLM generation. I've experimented with vector databases and different retrieval strategies while building my own AI applications.

## Q6. Do you have professional AI experience?

My professional experience is primarily in full-stack development, while most of my deeper GenAI experience comes from independently building and deploying AI projects. I've been consistently working with LLMs, RAG, agents, vector databases, and AI workflows to build production-style applications.

## Q7. How strong are you with DSA?

I have solved **300+ DSA problems** and maintained a **100+ day coding streak**. I continue practicing DSA to strengthen my problem-solving and interview skills.

## Q8. Do you prefer frontend or backend?

I'm comfortable with both. I started with frontend development and gradually moved deeper into backend development, databases, and system architecture. Currently, I'm particularly interested in the backend and AI infrastructure side of full-stack applications.

## Q9. How do you learn new technologies?

I usually learn by building. I first understand the fundamentals, read the official documentation, and then build a small project or feature around the technology. This helps me understand both the concepts and the practical trade-offs.

## Q10. What are you looking for in your next opportunity?

I'm looking for an environment where I can work on challenging engineering and AI problems, learn from experienced developers, and contribute to real products. I'm especially interested in teams building practical AI applications rather than AI features only as a surface-level addition.
